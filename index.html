4.2

Ideas

Each line is the idea; open it for the data behind it. These come from an outside view, to test against The5ers' own numbers.

Five ideas

1More creator collabsA regular programme with Indian trading creators, each with their own code.High▶
Three creator Reels with the code ARHAAN5 got 2.04M plays between them, 89% of all Reel plays on the account. The account itself has 7,932 followers, and its own Reels have a median of 2,061 plays.
Idea: a shortlist of Indian trading and finance creators, first-touch outreach, a code per creator, and one sheet that tracks plays, sign-ups and codes used.
2Every event as a seriesPosts before, during and after each tournament and conclave.High▶
Event posts have a median of 198 likes against 38 for the account. The Hyderabad conclave carousel got 496 likes, and event Reels got 20.7k to 73.7k plays. The Indore and cricket highlights show events are already part of the account's identity.
Idea: a set plan for every event: an announcement, a countdown, the day itself, and a recap with the traders who came.
3Trader stories in videoShort Hinglish clips from the funded-trader interviews.High▶
Nineteen trader stories in one static format have a median of 46 likes. A funded-trader interview is already linked in the bio on YouTube, so the footage exists.
Idea: 30 to 45-second clips cut from those interviews, with the payout on screen, posted as collabs with the trader where possible.
4Offers in Stories, proof on the gridOne current offer and code, kept in Stories and a highlight.Medium▶
Offer posts have a median of 34 likes and product updates 20. Black Friday and Cyber Monday got 15 likes each. In the Telegram community, traders ask for the current code every day.
Idea: a pinned Story highlight with the live offer, code and end date, so the grid can carry events, traders and payouts.
5A refreshed bioThe current offer first, plus a link to the Telegram community.Quick fix▶
The bio links are: Connect AI with The5ers, a funded-trader interview, The5ers Futures, the Summer Plan, and a Summer Plan breakdown. In October, two of the five are still for the Summer Plan, and none goes to the Telegram community.
Idea: the current offer at the top, then the Telegram community, YouTube and the main site, updated whenever an offer changes.
What already works
Events and tournaments that bring traders together in person, creator collabs, a consistent trader-story series, festival posts that feel local (Independence Day got 197 likes and Raksha Bandhan 195), and captions in English, Hinglish and Hindi.
